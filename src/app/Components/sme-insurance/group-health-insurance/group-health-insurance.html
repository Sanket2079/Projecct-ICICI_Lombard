What is group health insurance?

Group health insurance is a type of policy designed to cover a group of employees under one insurance plan.

It provides hospitalization coverage, cashless treatment, pre and post hospitalization expenses and several optional benefits depending on the policy.

Employers can also customize the plan according to organization requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Group Health Insurance?

Group Health Insurance is a health insurance policy purchased by an employer to provide medical coverage to employees under a single plan. It helps employees manage hospitalization expenses and offers financial protection against medical emergencies.

2. Who can purchase this policy?

Any registered company, startup, partnership firm, MSME, or organization can purchase a Group Health Insurance policy for its employees. The policy can be customized based on the number of employees and business requirements.

3. Does it include cashless treatment?

Yes. Employees can receive cashless treatment at a wide network of hospitals. The insurance company directly settles eligible medical expenses with the hospital, reducing the financial burden on employees.

4. Can family members be covered?

Yes. Employers can choose to extend coverage to employees' spouses, dependent children, and in some plans, dependent parents. The available options depend on the selected policy.

5. How can I raise a claim?

Claims can be raised through the insurer's online portal, mobile application, customer care, or by contacting the hospital during cashless treatment. Required documents should be submitted as per the claim process.
